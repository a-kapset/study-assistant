# The Brimlake Ferry Passenger Guide

This guide describes the ferry that crosses Brimlake, an invented mountain lake, between the north pier at Hollin and the south pier at Varrow Bay.

## 1. Tickets and boarding

A single crossing costs four brass tokens for an adult and two for a child under twelve. Tokens are sold at the kiosk beside each pier and on board by the purser. Bicycles travel free on the lower deck; a goat or a sheep needs its own child ticket. Boarding closes when the bell on the pier rings twice, five minutes before departure.

## 2. The daily crossings

The first crossing of the day leaves the north pier at 6:40 and reaches Varrow Bay at 7:25. After that a ferry leaves each pier every ninety minutes, and the last crossing leaves the south pier at 21:10. ## 3. Fog and storms

When fog hides the far shore, the ferry stays at the pier until the captain can see the lighthouse on Hollin Point. In a storm the purser hangs a red flag at both kiosks, and no ferry leaves until the flag is taken down. Waiting passengers may keep their tokens for a later day.
